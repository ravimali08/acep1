QUESTIONS?

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Can I upgrade my tier mid-month? expand_more
Absolutely! Upgrades are pro-rated — you only pay the difference for the remaining days. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
Is there a long-term contract? expand_more
No contracts. All memberships are month-to-month. Cancel anytime before your next billing date — no questions asked.
What's included in the Guest tier? expand_more
Guests get standard court access at $25/hour with equipment rental available for an additional fee. No advance booking — courts are subject to availability on the day.
How do Guest Passes work for Pro Elite? expand_more
Pro Elite members receive 2 complimentary guest passes per month. Each pass allows one guest to play for free during a single session. Unused passes don't roll over.
