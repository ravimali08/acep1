📋 GOOD TO KNOW

Booking Policies

⏰

24‑Hour Cancellation Policy

Cancel up to 24 hours before your booking for a full refund. Late cancellations are charged 50% of the booking fee.

⭐

Members Get Priority Booking

Club members can book courts up to 14 days in advance. Non‑members can book up to 7 days ahead.

🕐

Maximum Booking Duration

Non‑members can book a maximum of 2 hours per session. Members enjoy unlimited booking duration.

👟

Proper Court Etiquette

Non‑marking indoor shoes are required. Please arrive 5 minutes early and vacate promptly at the end of your session.
