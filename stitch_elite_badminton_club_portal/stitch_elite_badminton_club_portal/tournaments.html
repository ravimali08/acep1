RULES & FORMAT

Everything you need to know before competing. All tournaments follow BWF-sanctioned rules.

All Falcon Club tournaments follow a single-elimination bracket format with consolation rounds for first-round losers. Group stages may be used for events with 16+ participants. Matches are best of 3 games, each played to 21 points with a 2-point advantage rule. At 29-all, the first player to reach 30 wins the game. Seedings are based on current season rankings.

Registration is open to all Falcon Club members in good standing. Non-members may participate in select open events with an additional $20 facility fee. Registration closes 48 hours before the tournament start date. Players must check in at least 30 minutes before their first scheduled match. A valid photo ID is required at check-in. Age-restricted events require proof of age.

All matches follow BWF (Badminton World Federation) official rules. Rally point scoring system is used. A 60-second interval is allowed between each game, and an interval of 2 minutes between the second and third games. Players change ends after each game and when the leading score reaches 11 in the final game. Only BWF-approved feather shuttlecocks are used in all sanctioned events.

Champions receive a trophy, medal, and cash prize (varies by tournament). Runner-up and semifinalists receive medals and ranking points. The Falcon Cup features the largest prize pool of $5,000 total, split across all divisions. Season-end awards include MVP, Most Improved, and Sportsmanship awards. Top-ranked players at season end earn priority registration for next season's events.

Proper badminton attire is required — athletic sportswear in appropriate colors. Non-marking court shoes are mandatory; no running shoes, sandals, or casual footwear allowed. Players must bring their own rackets; the club provides shuttlecocks for all official matches. Team events require matching team jerseys or shirts. The tournament director has final authority on attire disputes.

All participants are expected to demonstrate sportsmanship and respect. Verbal abuse, racket throwing, and intimidation are grounds for immediate disqualification. Disputes are resolved by the tournament referee whose decision is final. Players must be ready to play within 5 minutes of being called; failure to appear results in a walkover. Anti-doping policies are in effect for all sanctioned events.
