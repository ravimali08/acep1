FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cancellation policy? +
We believe in flexibility. You can cancel your membership at any time with no cancellation fees. Monthly memberships can be cancelled before your next billing cycle. Annual memberships can be cancelled with a prorated refund for unused months (minus a small processing fee of $25). Simply visit the front desk or email us at membership@falconclub.com to initiate cancellation.
Can I freeze my membership? +
Absolutely! Life happens, and we get it. You can freeze your membership for up to 3 months per year at no charge. During the freeze period, your billing is paused and your membership tier is preserved. To freeze your account, provide at least 7 days' notice before your next billing date. Extended freezes beyond 3 months may be available for medical reasons with documentation.
Do you offer family plans? +
Yes! Families that play together stay together. We offer family bundles at a 15% discount when 3 or more family members sign up on the same plan. Each family member gets their own individual membership benefits. Junior memberships (ages 8–17) are available at 40% off adult pricing. Contact our membership team for a customized family package tailored to your needs.
Is there a free trial period? +
Yes! We offer a 7-day free trial for all new members. During your trial, you'll have full access to Bronze-level amenities, including 2 court sessions, group classes, and locker room facilities. No credit card is required to start your trial. If you love it (and we're sure you will!), you can upgrade to any tier seamlessly. Walk into our club or sign up online to begin your trial today.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan? +
Of course! You can change your membership tier at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, and you'll be charged the prorated difference for the remainder of your billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Simply visit the front desk or log into your member portal to make changes.
